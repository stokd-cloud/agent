### Limits and defaults
- One coordinator lease per agent; FIFO ordinary ingress; host heartbeat every 10 seconds and lease expiry at 45 seconds using server time. Lease expiry is infrastructure liveness, not user scheduling.
- Coordinator context input cap is the smaller of 32,000 tokens and the model capacity minus tool schemas/output reserve. Reserve at least 4,096 output tokens. Pinned policy, identity, commitments relevant to the turn and current prompt are never silently truncated; overflow produces a named error or requests a smaller input.
- Maximum 20 model steps and 10 minutes per coordinator wake; continuation requires a recorded new wake. Ordinary dispatch uses at most one executor per work request; user-authorized multiple work requests remain distinct. No recursive standalone worker spawning.
- SSE is at-least-once transport with durable monotonically ordered conversation events and client deduplication. Model output may stream as provisional; success becomes final only after durable commit.
- Artifacts up to 5 GiB use resumable multipart uploads; avatar up to 10 MiB and raster PNG/JPEG/WebP only. Per-agent default storage quota is 10 GiB, configurable by the operator; it fails explicitly when exceeded.
- Upload-only presigned grants expire within 5 minutes and bind fixed parts/lengths/checksums of a server-created multipart session, even for small files. Only the server can complete it; no client PutObject or CompleteMultipartUpload permission exists. Already-issued grants can rewrite only their pending part until expiry/abort. Finalization verifies bytes and pins S3 VersionId before publication; replay cannot create completed versions or change ready content. Downloads stream through the authenticated Agent API, never through a presigned S3 download URL; authorization/tombstone checks stop an open stream within 30 seconds, and cannot recall bytes already delivered. HTML/SVG/script documents download as inert attachments; no active terminal/browser execution.
- Normal standalone sessions expire after 7 days and are revocable per host. Stokd-derived sessions expire within 5 minutes and never outlive their source session; refresh re-attests the source session, and authenticated introspection caches positive results for at most 15 seconds. Source revocation/expiry denies derived commands, streams, leases and pending actions within 30 seconds; inability to establish freshness fails closed. Direct Agent-session revocation rejects the next command and live access within 30 seconds.
- Production backups: daily DB backup retained 30 days, S3 versioning and 30-day deleted-version retention; restore to an isolated environment must prove referential consistency. Archive hides an agent, blocks admission/dispatch, cancels its active coordinator and disables automatic result continuation; it is not immediate erasure. Memory forget excludes retrieval immediately and tombstones source/derivation links against automatic resurrection; retained transcripts/backups are disclosed.
